On call tonight

The pager goes off. What do you check first?

You will learn: how to work an incident from symptom to cause, one decision at a time, on three of the most common database pages.

Each incident starts with an alert. At every step you pick what to do, and you are told whether it was a good call, a workable one, or one that would make things worse, and why. The outputs are written to look like the real commands, and they are samples, not captured runs. The causes are the ones the rest of this site measures and explains.

For the real thing, the first five minutes checklist and the on-call reading path are the pages to keep open.
